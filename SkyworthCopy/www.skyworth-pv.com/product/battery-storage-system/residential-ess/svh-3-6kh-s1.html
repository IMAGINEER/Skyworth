SWH 3-6KH-S1 Single Phase High Voltage Hybrid Inverter

Get the Product

View Video

Add to Wishlist

  • Higher Efficiency
    Higher Generation
    2 MPPTs

    The Solavita 3–6kW hybrid inverter supports 2 MPPT inputs, ideal for PV systems with two roof orientations. It allows up to 150% PV oversizing to boost solar generation, while its high battery charge/discharge efficiency ensures more usable power is delivered to the load, maximizing solar self-consumption and system performance.

  • 150% oversizing
  • Max. 97% discharge efficiency
  • Safety and Reliability

    The Solavita 3–6kW hybrid inverter features built-in Type II AC/DC surge protection and an IP66-rated enclosure, making it ideal for reliable outdoor installation. With a fast 10ms seamless switchover, the system ensures uninterrupted power supply during grid outages, providing stable backup power for critical home loads.

    • Type II AC/DC SPD

    • 10ms seamless switchover

    • IP66 rating

  • Smarter and User-friendly

    The Solavita 3–6kW hybrid inverter offers 24/7 local and remote monitoring, enabling real-time system visibility. Its compact and lightweight design allows for one-person installation, while the fanless cooling system minimizes operating noise and reduces maintenance costs.

    • LCD and APP operation

    • Compact design

    • Fanless design
