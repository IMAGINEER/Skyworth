Project Overview

Solavita proudly presents our Residential Energy Storage System (ESS) project in Frankfurt, Germany. This innovative project features a 12kW hybrid inverter paired with a 14kWh battery. By integrating advanced energy storage technology, we empower local residents to harness solar power efficiently, significantly reducing reliance on the grid. This initiative not only cuts down electricity costs but also plays a crucial role in promoting green energy and sustainability in the Frankfurt area.
