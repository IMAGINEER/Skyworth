SWM800-R · SWM1600-R

Railing Mounting

Designed for open balconies in villas and apartments. The J-shaped hook attaches cleanly to balcony railings without drilling. Bauhaus foldable support simplifies installation and storage — the entire system packs flat for easy transportation.

  • String-style frame with rounded corners (collision prevention)
  • Foldable high-strength support — no tools required
  • High resistance to wind and snow
  • Plug-and-play AC connection
  • Hinge-connected module and support for cleaner profile
  • Serrated pattern · Rounded hook · Texture splicing design

Best for: Villa balconies · Apartment balconies · Utility balconies

SWM800-S · SWM1600-S

Sunshade Mounting

Functions as both a solar generator and a sunshade canopy. The all-steel support provides a stable structure with excellent wind and snow resistance. All-black minimalist design (without grid lines) blends naturally with shop fronts, windowsill facades, and residential entrances.

  • All-steel support with enhanced wind and snow resistance
  • All-black minimalist design (no grid lines)
  • High-power anti-microcrack modules
  • Plug-and-play installation
  • Dual function: power generation + sun protection

Best for: Shop fronts · Windowsill exterior facades · Self-built house entrances

SWM800-G · SWM1600-G

Ground Mounting

The most versatile option — folds flat for easy storage and deploys in three tool-free steps. Four adjustable angle positions (25°, 30°, 35°, 40°) let you capture maximum sunlight in any season or location. Compact enough for courtyards, gardens, or even camping sites.

  • Minimalist design — folds flat for easy storage
  • Tool-free three-step installation
  • Four adjustable angles: 25° / 30° / 35° / 40°
  • Support slotted slideway for fine-tuning
  • Plug-and-play AC connection

Best for: Villa courtyard · Self-built house courtyard · Camping
